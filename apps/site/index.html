What it does

Everything in the box.

11 features · MIT

01

Tasks on the grid

Your agent schedules a task through the API and it shows up on the calendar. That's the whole reason this exists.

02

Week grid

Double click or drag to create. Right click menus. A real edit form.

03

Unlimited calendars

Local calendars and recurring events. No Google needed, and add as many as you want.

04

Kanban board

Four columns: Backlog, In Progress, Check, Done. Drag cards around.

05

63 MCP tools

Everything your agent could want, over one API key.

06

2ndBrain link

Two way sync between Obsidian Tasks lines and your tasks. Dry run first, backup before it writes.

07

Google sync

Automatic and both ways, across any number of accounts, with a picker per calendar.

08

Booking pages

Share a link at /book/your-name with your availability and approvals.

09

Routines, tags, subtasks

Plus a command bar and full keyboard control.

10

Reminders

By push, email and Telegram.
